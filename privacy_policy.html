TO-DO - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the TO-DO application collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

Information We Collect

When you use the TO-DO application, we may collect information that is necessary to provide account and application functionality.

  • Email address: Used to create, manage, and authenticate your account.
  • Account information: Information associated with your TO-DO account may be stored to provide application functionality.
  • Task information: Tasks and related information that you create in the application may be stored so that they are available to you.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected by the TO-DO application may be used for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To authenticate your account.
  • To provide the application's TO-DO functionality.
  • To store and display your tasks.
  • To maintain and improve the application.
  • To respond to support or privacy-related requests.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may use service providers or third-party services necessary to operate the application. These services may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle information securely.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain account and application data for as long as necessary to provide the TO-DO application and its functionality.

When an account deletion request is verified and processed, the associated account and applicable user data will be deleted in accordance with our account deletion process.
